## Creation brief and final prompts

Portrait creation brief: Liza is a Filipino senior high school student with shoulder-length straight dark hair, thin round dark glasses, warm tan skin and dark brown eyes. White short-sleeve school blouse with green pocket stripe and navy waist/skirt. Six consistent waist-up poses in an equal 3×2 transparent atlas, each contained in its cell. Match established anime/painterly school-story artwork with subtle pixel texture. No text, floor, halo or watermark.

Final portrait edit prompt:

> Edit this exact six-pose Liza sprite sheet. Remove ALL the brown/gray gradient background and glows between and behind the six figures. Output genuinely transparent alpha everywhere outside the characters, with clean hard cutout edges, no shadow or halo. Preserve the exact 1536x1024 canvas and equal 3-column 2-row grid, character identities, faces, clothes, poses and positions. Each figure remains fully inside its own cell. This is a game sprite atlas, not a poster. Background must be transparent, not drawn checkerboard.

Title prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Production mobile visual novel title background, landscape 16:9. A tense cinematic illustration in the established LEVELBLUE school cybersecurity mystery style, clean anime linework, painterly cel shading and subtle crisp pixel texture. Liza is a Filipino senior high school student with warm tan skin, shoulder-length straight black hair with center part, thin round dark glasses, dark brown eyes, white short sleeve collared school blouse with a dark green stripe on the breast pocket and navy skirt. Place her on the right-center of the composition in a covered school courtyard on a rainy afternoon, holding a dark phone near her ear, looking down in worried concentration at its screen light reflected in her glasses. Wet courtyard tiles, rain beyond columns, distant school windows and soft teal light, realistic coherent school architecture. Mystery is in the anonymous call, no visible villain, no extra characters. Navy #050B18 shadows, muted teal/cyan highlights, restrained warm window light. A faint abstract audio-wave motif in the dark negative space on the left, integrated into the atmosphere rather than a UI. Lower left dark and quiet for title text to be added by the game. Top right and bottom edges leave room for mobile controls. High quality and dramatic but grounded, no horror, no gore. NO words, lettering, logos, watermarks, buttons or baked UI. Wide landscape.

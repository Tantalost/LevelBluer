### Mystery title

Create a striking landscape 16:9 mystery visual-novel chapter title key art for LEVELBLUE Module 2, social-engineering via text messages. Polished hand-painted anime illustration, crisp expressive linework, subtle pixel-texture finish consistent with a school mystery game. Amani is a fictional Filipina senior high student: warm medium-brown skin, brown almond eyes, dark black low ponytail with loose side strands, small teal hairclip on the viewer-right side of her hair, white short-sleeve school blouse with dark green pocket accent, navy lanyard and skirt. Depict her in the center-right at three-quarter angle holding a smartphone close to her chest, looking uneasily over her shoulder; cool cyan phone light illuminates her face and fingers, warm sliver of light on her hair. Behind her, a school corridor dissolves into deep navy shadow; abstract translucent rectangular message fragments and a distorted duplicate of her profile silhouette suggest impersonation, not a literal second person. A tiny broken reflection in dark glass and thin cyan interference lines add suspense. No literal hacker villain, no known culprit, no masks, no weapons, no gore. Cinematic strong diagonal composition, pronounced depth and chiaroscuro, dark atmospheric navy #050B18 / #0A1730 with muted teal #4FE0D4 and tiny warm amber highlights. Character occupies central 45–78% width, face near center vertically, not extreme edges. Keep upper-right 25%, upper-left 25%, bottom-left 45%, and bottom-right 24% dark and low-detail for the game's MODULE 2 label, title and controls. Full-bleed image with no text, letters, numbers, logos, button graphics, framing or title embedded. This is a mysterious narrative key art, not a normal classroom screenshot, not a sprite on a plain background.
